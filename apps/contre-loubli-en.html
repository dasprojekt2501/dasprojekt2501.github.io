About the App

Contre L'oubli is a personal journaling app that turns daily entries, marked quotes, moods, and Oura Ring health data into material for AI-generated reflections.

It treats a journal not only as a log, but as a place where memory, sleep, recovery, and the rhythms of the body can become material for thought.
