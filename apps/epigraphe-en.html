About

Save the words you want to keep, and one of them arrives each day.

An épigraphe is the short quotation set at the beginning of a book.

This app really lives in its widgets and its notifications. The screens are only the back room. A notification here is not an invitation to open the app. It is the thing itself, finished where you read it.

The day decides the card. On the same day, the widget, the notification and the app all show the same one. The order is not random. Cards are drawn so that the collection turns over as a whole, which keeps the same few lines from crowding out the rest. Something you had forgotten comes back once you have forgotten it.

Features

  • Today's card — chosen by the date, the same for that day
  • Home Screen widgets (medium and large)
  • Lock Screen widgets
  • Per-widget scope: all cards, favorites only, or one deck
  • A daily notification, at an hour you choose, with optional drift
  • Import a text file in one go
  • Save from other apps with the share button
  • An attribution line like "— Name, Source" is filed automatically
  • Decks, favorites, search, and duplicate checking
  • Share as text or as an image, and save the image to Photos
  • My Reminders — your own lines, at a time you set
  • JSON export and restore (everything, one deck, or favorites)
  • Japanese and English

How it was made

It was built on one rule: an app for not opening. Every feature added pulls it further from one line a day, so when in doubt, nothing was added.

No advertising. No account.

This app does not connect to anything. There is not a single line of networking code in it. What you save is not sent to a server, and I cannot read it either.

You can export whenever you like: everything as JSON, or one deck, or only your favorites. Read it back and the favorites and dates return with it.
