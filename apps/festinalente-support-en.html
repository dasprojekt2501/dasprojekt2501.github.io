Frequently Asked Questions

⏱ How do I change the durations or number of rounds?

Tap the gear button at the top right of the main screen to open Settings, then use the − and + buttons for Work, Rest, Rounds, and Ready. Work can be set from 5 seconds to 10 minutes, rest from 0 seconds to 5 minutes, and rounds from 1 to 99, or ∞ to repeat until you stop. Settings can be changed while the timer is not running and are saved automatically.

⏸ How do I pause, stop, or skip?

While the timer is running, tap the center button or the dial itself to pause and resume. The right button skips the current work or rest period. To prevent accidental stops, the stop button on the left can only be pressed while paused.

🔊 There is no sound

Check that your device volume is turned up. The app plays its sounds even in silent mode. Also make sure Sound is turned on in the app's Settings.

🎵 Can I use it with music or videos?

Yes. The signal sounds play on top of music or videos from other apps without stopping them.

🔒 What happens when I lock the screen?

While the timer is running, the screen will not turn off by itself. If you lock the screen or switch to another app, no signal sounds play in the meantime. When you return, the timer continues from the correct position based on the time that has passed.

🌐 How do I change the language?

Tap JP or EN at the top left of the Settings screen to switch between Japanese and English.

📱 Does it work in landscape?

Yes. Turn your iPhone sideways for a landscape layout, with the dial on the left and the round count and controls on the right.
