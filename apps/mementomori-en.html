About the App

Memento Mori — Remember death.

The Stoic philosophers of ancient Rome taught: never forget that you will die.

This app quietly and precisely visualizes the time you have left.

Set your birth date and life's endpoint. Watch your remaining days count down in real time.

Key Features

  • Real-time countdown of remaining days and hours
  • Life progress bar
  • Full-screen countdown display
  • Life in Weeks grid — see your entire life at a glance
  • Year Progress — visualize how much of this year has passed
  • Home screen and Lock Screen widgets
  • Daily reminder notifications
  • Birthday milestone notifications
  • Japanese and English support

Design Philosophy

Nothing unnecessary.

No ads. No account required.

Your data stays on your device — nothing is ever sent externally.

Contemplating death is not despair. It is a philosophy for truly living today.
