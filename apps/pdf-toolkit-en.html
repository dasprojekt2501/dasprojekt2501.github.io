Select one PDF to redact or blur
Redaction: Replaces selected areas with black pixels and rasterizes every page into a new PDF. Original text, images, links, forms, scripts, and embedded data are not copied. Always inspect the output before sharing it.
Blur is not redaction. Blurred content may still be inferred or reconstructed. Always use Redact for personal data, passwords, or confidential information.
